## Accuracy, and the limits of what we publish

Events are rescheduled, prices change, societies change their contacts, representatives leave office, and businesses close. Information on UniSpace may be incomplete or out of date.

Some content is submitted by students, organisers or business owners. Where we review or verify something, that review covers only the specific checks we describe — nothing more.

We label content by how well it is checked. A student tip is a report of someone's experience, not a statement of fact that we have confirmed. Nothing on UniSpace is an official university announcement.

**For admissions, academic programmes, entry requirements, institutional policies and official announcements, use the relevant university's own website.** We link to those sites for that reason.

Please confirm time-sensitive details — dates, venues, prices, opening hours — with the organiser, business or institution before relying on them.
