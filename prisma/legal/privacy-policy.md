UniSpace is an independent student-life and campus information platform. It is not operated or endorsed by the universities it features.

This policy describes what UniSpace actually does with information today. Where a feature has not been released yet, this policy says so, and it is updated before that feature goes live.

## Browsing without an account

You do not need an account to browse UniSpace. There is no public sign-up, and we do not ask visitors for a name, an email address or a student number in order to read anything on the platform.

Administrator accounts are the only accounts on UniSpace, and they are created by invitation only.

## Information we collect

### When you browse

Our hosting and database providers process technical information needed to deliver the pages you request, such as the request URL, the time of the request, your browser's user-agent string and your IP address. This is ordinary server operation; we do not combine it into a profile of you.

UniSpace does not currently use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, this policy and the cookie information below are updated first.

### When you vote in a poll

Campus polls are open to the public and do not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or student number in order to vote.

To make repeat voting harder, a short-lived identifier scoped to the individual poll is stored in your browser, and the vote record holds only a keyed hash derived from it. That hash is not reversible to you, is never returned by a public part of the site, and is not linked to any account.

This is an anti-abuse measure, not a guarantee. Anonymous public voting cannot perfectly prevent someone from voting more than once — for example by using a different browser or device. We therefore describe polls as informal community polls rather than representative surveys, and we do not present their results as official university decisions or as a statistically representative measure of student opinion.

### When you send us something

Some parts of UniSpace let you send information to us: suggesting a campus tip, event, tour stop or correction; applying to list a campus business; submitting an advertising enquiry; reporting content; or contacting us. Each of those forms collects only what is needed for that purpose, and tells you so next to the form.

Where a form asks for contact details, it explains why. We use them to follow up on what you sent, and for nothing else.

### When you administer content

Administrator accounts hold a name, an email address, a hashed password (we never store the password itself), and the role and campus assignments that determine what the account may do.

For security, we record administrator sessions and sensitive administrative actions. These records include the time, the account involved, what changed, the browser's user-agent string, and a short non-reversible value derived from the IP address — not the address itself.

## Why we process information

- To operate the platform and show you the pages you ask for.
- To review submissions before anything is published.
- To respond to enquiries, corrections and reports.
- To process campus business verification applications.
- To manage advertising enquiries.
- To protect against spam, abuse, manipulation of polls, and security incidents.
- To maintain, debug and improve the platform.
- To meet legal obligations that apply to us.

## Cookies and similar technologies

UniSpace uses the following, and nothing else:

- **An administrator session cookie.** Set only when an administrator signs in. It is strictly necessary for the dashboard to work, is marked HttpOnly and SameSite, and is marked Secure in production.
- **A poll ballot identifier.** Set in your browser when you vote, scoped to that poll, and used only to make repeat voting harder.

We do not set advertising cookies, and we do not use third-party analytics cookies.

## Maps on our pages

Some pages — the virtual campus tour, and business listings with a confirmed location — show a map supplied by Google Maps.

That map is loaded by your browser directly from Google. When it loads, Google receives your IP address and other ordinary request information, and may set its own cookies in your browser, under Google's privacy policy rather than ours. We do not send Google your name, your email address or anything you have typed into UniSpace, and we do not receive anything back about you.

Pages without a map make no request to Google. Maps are loaded lazily, so a map further down a page is only fetched if you scroll to it.

## Sharing information

We share information only with the service providers needed to run UniSpace — our hosting provider, our database provider, and, where used, an email delivery provider and an image storage provider. They process it on our instructions.

We do not sell personal information.

Submitting an advertising enquiry does not give an advertiser access to any visitor's information. Advertisers receive no personal data about visitors through UniSpace.

We may disclose information where we are legally required to do so, or where it is necessary to investigate abuse or protect the security of the platform.

## How long we keep information

- **Administrator session records:** removed or revoked when a session expires or an administrator signs out.
- **Submissions, applications and enquiries:** kept while they are being handled and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can answer follow-up questions and show a decision history.
- **Audit records of administrative actions:** kept longer, because their purpose is accountability.
- **Poll ballot hashes:** kept for the life of the poll's results.

Retention periods are set against operational need and the law that applies to us. We do not promise a period we cannot keep to, and we delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.

## Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to object to or restrict how we use it.

To make a request, use the privacy contact address published on our Contact page. We may need to ask you for information to confirm who you are before we act, and some requests have limits — for example, we may keep records we are required to keep.

## Security

We protect information with measures appropriate to its sensitivity: administrator passwords are stored only as hashes, session tokens are stored only as hashes, access to records is restricted by role and by campus, private submissions are not exposed through public parts of the site, and sensitive administrative actions are logged.

No service can promise absolute security, and we do not.

## Processing outside Malawi

Our service providers may process information on servers outside Malawi. Where that happens, we rely on the provider's contractual commitments to protect it.

## Children

UniSpace is intended for university students and the wider campus community. It is not directed at children.
